First time? Start here

You need Jellyfin Server with your library and the Vesper Keep player running on your computer.

The downloadable Vesper Keep setup is still being prepared. This preview works with an already-installed player.

  1. Choose Open my player, then Connect Jellyfin.
  2. Keep the code visible and choose Open your Jellyfin to approve.
  3. Sign in to Jellyfin. Open your profile menu, choose Quick Connect, enter the code, and confirm.
  4. Return to the player page. Your library opens automatically.
  5. Join the Keep in VRChat, choose Pair my in-world menu on the player page, then copy the full link into the Jellyfin tab.
  6. Choose Connect library. Browse your catalogue and press Play.
What gets shared?

Connecting Jellyfin lets you browse your own library. Pairing connects its catalogue to your current Keep instance so people there can browse it and choose what to watch, subject to the screen’s controls.

Choose Stop pairing on your player page to remove catalogue access. Leaving the Keep also ends pairing. Use the video player’s Stop button to stop the current video.

Your sign-in stays in Jellyfin.
Vesper Keep never receives your password. Temporary access stays in memory and is cleared when you disconnect.
