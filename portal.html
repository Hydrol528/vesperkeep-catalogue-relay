First time? Start here

You need Jellyfin Server set up with your movies or shows, the Vesper Keep player running on your computer, and VRChat open in the Keep. Keep the computer running while you watch.

Install Vesper Keep Player

  1. Choose Download for Windows · Preview above. Open the downloaded VesperKeepPlayer-0.1.2-preview.msi file.
  2. Follow the setup steps and choose where to install it. Setup adds a Vesper Keep Player shortcut to your desktop.
  3. Start Jellyfin Server, then open Vesper Keep Player using that shortcut. In the app, choose Open player. Keep both programs running while watching.

Need Jellyfin first? Get Jellyfin Server and add your own movies or shows to its library. The separate Jellyfin desktop viewing app is optional.

Windows preview: playback has been tested with the player and VRChat on the same PC. Streaming movies to standalone Quest or another home is not ready.

To uninstall: close Vesper Keep Player using Stop and exit, then open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Vesper Keep Player → Uninstall. Your Jellyfin library and movie files stay where they are.

Connect Jellyfin

  1. At the top of this page, choose Open my player. Your player page opens in a new browser tab.
  2. Choose Connect Jellyfin. The player shows a six-digit code. Leave that tab open.
  3. Choose Open Jellyfin. Jellyfin opens in another tab. Sign in to your Jellyfin account if asked.
  4. In Jellyfin, choose your profile icon in the top-right corner, then choose Quick Connect.
  5. Enter the six-digit code from the player page and approve the connection.
  6. Return to the player tab. Your library appears automatically; you do not need to enter the code again.

Show your catalogue in the Keep

  1. On the player page, choose Pair my in-world menu, then Copy full link.
  2. Switch to VRChat and join the Keep. Go to the theatre and select the small round logo below the theatre screen to open its menu.
  3. Select the Jellyfin tab, then select the pairing link field. An Enter URL box opens.
  4. Replace the text in that box with your copied link. On a computer, use Ctrl+V to paste. Submit it with the blue arrow, then select Connect library.
  5. Your catalogue’s posters appear. Select a movie, or a series followed by its season and episode. Select Play.

Finished? Use Stop to stop the video. Choose Stop pairing on your player page to remove catalogue access. Leaving the Keep also ends pairing.

What gets shared?

Connecting Jellyfin lets you browse your own library. Pairing connects its catalogue to your current Keep instance so people there can browse it and choose what to watch, subject to the screen’s controls.

Choose Stop pairing on your player page to remove catalogue access. Leaving the Keep also ends pairing. Use the video player’s Stop button to stop the current video.

About the app and its source

Vesper Keep Player is a Windows helper, separate from Jellyfin. Its source code, privacy details, and build workflow are public. Download checksums and build-origin information are available on its release page. Build-origin information shows where an installer was built; it is not a security guarantee.

Your sign-in stays in Jellyfin.
Vesper Keep never receives your password. Temporary access stays in memory and is cleared when you disconnect.
